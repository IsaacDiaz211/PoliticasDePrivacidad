Privacy Policy
Leyendolo
Last Updated: March 16, 2026

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Leyendolo handles data.

Leyendolo is operated by an independent developer: Isaac Diaz.
The app is designed with a minimal-data approach and does not collect or store personal data from end users.

2. Data We Collect
We do not collect or store personal data from end users.

Specifically, Leyendolo does not use user accounts, does not maintain user profiles, and does not keep a user database.
We do not retain the content users submit in prompts, text inputs, or uploaded material.

3. Limited Technical Metadata
For service operation and cost control, the backend stores limited request metadata for OpenRouter calls:

- date and time of the request
- model used
- token usage

This metadata does not include user content and is not used to identify individual users.

4. Infrastructure Logs and Retention
The backend runs on Vercel Hobby.
Platform logs are transient and available only for a limited technical window (up to approximately 30 minutes).
We do not keep separate copies of those logs and we do not use them to identify users.

5. How We Use Technical Metadata
The limited metadata described above is used only to:

- keep the service running
- monitor usage and costs
- diagnose operational issues

6. Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell personal data.

To provide the service, we rely on third-party providers such as Vercel (hosting) and OpenRouter (AI gateway).
Only the minimum technical data required for service delivery is processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. International Processing
Service infrastructure may process technical data in countries other than your own, including the United States.

8. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have privacy rights.
You can contact us at isaac211.md@proton.md to submit a request.

Because Leyendolo does not maintain user accounts or identifiable user records, we may not be able to link requests to a specific person in many cases.

9. Children's Privacy
Leyendolo is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will publish the updated version in the app and revise the "Last Updated" date.
